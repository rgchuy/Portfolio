Bringing podcasts to TREBEL

In 2021, podcast listening was growing fast. TREBEL added podcasts to reach new audiences and open room for new products. Many of its users have entry-level phones, little storage and small data plans, so every screen was designed around those limits.

  • Entry-level devices

    Light, list-based screens that stay fast on low-memory phones.

  • Limited data

    Duration, progress and status are visible before anything is streamed.

  • Familiar patterns

    Podcasts reuse TREBEL’s home rows, lists, action sheets and player.

Long story short

Rather than launching as a standalone section, podcasts were integrated into the existing product architecture. Listeners could access them through the home screen, the library and the player, which keeps the learning curve minimal and builds on established user behavior.

The design strategy prioritized precise, purposeful details that reduce interaction steps, data consumption and user uncertainty. These include persistent playback progress on every episode, clear filtering options and lightweight action sheets.

01 · Home

Podcasts on the home screen

A “Your favorite Podcast” row sits between music rows, using the same card pattern, so podcasts are discovered without learning a new section.

  • 1Now-playing disc. While an episode plays, its cover spins at the edge of the screen. One tap returns to the player from anywhere on home.
  • 2Shared card pattern. Large covers with title and creator make shows recognizable at a glance, with very little reading.
1 2

02 · Show page

Follow once, find it again

The show page puts the cover, title and network first, followed by a single clear action.

  • 1Follow / Following. The button changes state immediately and sends the show to the library, next to Music and Videos.
  • 2Tabs for depth. “In this podcast” and “Description” keep long show notes out of the way until they are needed.

03 · Episodes

Filters in one tap

Tapping the filter row opens a short action sheet from the bottom, within thumb reach, with the options users need most: all, unplayed and played episodes.

  • Tap filter
  • Action sheet
  • Unplayed only
  • ✓Always reversible. When a filter is active, a “remove filter” option appears at the bottom, so returning to the default list never requires searching.

Usability details

Small details, real benefits

1

Fixed text length

Titles stop at three lines and descriptions at two. Every card keeps a predictable height, so long lists stay easy to scan.

2

Progress is saved

A progress bar and “30min left” show exactly where listening stopped, so episodes resume instead of streaming again.

3

Played marker

Finished episodes are tagged “Played”, preventing accidental replays and wasted data.

4

Play from the list

Each card has its own play button and shows date and duration first, so users judge time and data cost before tapping.

Outcome

Podcasts became part of TREBEL’s home, library and player, built from existing components and designed around low-end devices, limited storage and small data plans.
