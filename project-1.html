Rethinking the Betting Experience

A walk through how betting was redesigned for sportsbook players: mapping the old flow, rebuilding it around a single flexible screen, and carrying it to mobile and brand color themes.

Long story short

PayPerHead provides sportsbook software that lets bookies manage their agents and betting lines, and run a branded betting site for each agent’s players.

The betting sites were outdated and hard to use, weakening player retention and the brands’ credibility. The redesign replaced a rigid, step-by-step betting flow with a flexible one, shaped by user feedback, business goals and the technical limitations of external providers.

The result is one screen for the whole bet, a better mobile experience and a wider variety of color themes for every brand.

A simpler way to place bets

The step-by-step wizard, where going back erased all progress, was replaced by three columns working side by side: sports, lines and the betslip. Bettors can adjust any of them independently, at any moment, without losing the selections they have already made.

What was removed and where it went

Nothing was lost. Each part of the wizard became something visible on the page, changeable at any time and usable in any order.

Platform refinements

Easier to navigate, easier to brand

Header reorganization

The header was consolidated from two crowded rows into a single row organized by function. This freed space for betting content and created a clearer hierarchy, allowing players to locate each action more quickly.

Navigation menu reorganization

The old burger menu showed many products at once in one long list. The menu was reorganized by function: products now live in the bottom bar, within thumb reach, while personal and account information sits in a profile panel at the top. Each category has a clear place, so players always know where to find what they need.

Minimize decision fatigue

Easy modification,
all in one place

The old flow made players pick a bet type before seeing any game, and every change meant starting over. That repetition led to fatigue.

The new betslip keeps every pick at hand, with the flexibility to switch between bet types and picks, adapting to what each bet type needs, without leaving the screen.

Real color theme personalization

Requests for more customization kept reaching the customer service team: agents wanted color to play a real role in their sites. Before, a theme could only switch between black and white, and a barely visible hero color. Now color is deeply integrated into theme customization, carrying each brand’s palette through buttons, selections, tabs and the betslip.
