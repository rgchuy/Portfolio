Bringing podcasts to TREBEL

Podcast listening grew in 2021, opening room for new products and new audiences. The format was brought into TREBEL as a native experience, designed around the constraints its users face, such as limited data and phone storage.

Long story short

As TREBEL grew beyond music downloads, new content and features had to fit into an app people already knew.

The design focused on integrating podcasts, refining the details that mattered most to TREBEL’s audience while staying within the existing design system, so the new content felt native to the app from its first release.

Key flows

From discovery to listening

01 · Podcast introduction

Podcast integration

Podcasts were integrated into the existing app structure: shows appear between the music rows on the home screen, each one opens its own show page, and a new Podcasts section in the Library brings everything together in one place.

02 · Filtering and sorting

Details that give a better experience

Episodes can be filtered and sorted to match each listener’s needs, while progress and played indicators show how far into every episode they are.

Filter and sort

  1. Tap the filter icon
  2. Choose “Played” in the action sheet
  3. Only played episodes are listed
  4. “Remove filter” brings every episode back in one tap
  • Ready to play Date, full duration and a direct way to start listening
  • In progress Time left and a progress bar, so listeners resume where they stopped
  • Played Marked with a check, with the option to play it again

03 · Library rebuild

One library for every kind of media

The library was rebuilt to hold music, videos and podcasts, each with its own content inside, such as followed shows, downloaded and played episodes for podcasts. Several layouts were explored before arriving at a single structure that scales across every type of media.

Storage

Saving space after listening

Played episodes can be deleted automatically to free up phone storage. A call to action in the Downloaded library leads straight to podcast settings, where a single toggle removes each episode 48 hours after it has been played.

Player

A player made for podcasts

The music player was adapted to the needs of podcasts, adding practical tools that give listeners more control over long episodes: when to stop, how fast to listen and how to keep playing while reading the episode notes.
